# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want openborld to be a project where contributing is judged on the work, and
where asking a basic question is as welcome as sending a large patch. We pledge
to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique code, not people. "This leaks a file handle" is useful; "this is
  sloppy" is not.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A rejected PR is a scoping
  decision, not a judgement of you.
- When you disagree, say what you'd do instead and why.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory
or by direct message. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for
behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement is
proportionate: a first lapse usually gets a private word, not a ban.
